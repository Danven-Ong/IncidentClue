# Collecting evidence without asserting a cause

## Symptoms

Only a generic error-rate increase or a nearby deployment timestamp is available.

## Mechanism

Symptoms can have multiple explanations. Timing alone cannot establish causation. Explicit contradictory observations must remain visible rather than being discarded to fit a pattern.

## Checks

Request error categories, phase timings and matching health/log observations. If these are absent, describe missing evidence and retain an insufficient-evidence conclusion.
